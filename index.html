A course to make you ensemble-ready

Blackjack Ensemble — Learn the Repo, Practise TDD

Short, hands-on lessons that take you from “I just cloned it” to confidently pairing on Ted Young's blackjack-ensemble-blue. Do one lesson, complete its task, say “done”, and I'll test you before we move on.

The mission When it's your turn at the keyboard in the mob, you know where code lives, why the tests are shaped the way they are, and how to drive a change red→green→refactor without slowing the team down. See MISSION.md.

How this works

  • Each lesson is one HTML page: a little knowledge, quick recall quizzes, then a hands-on task in the real repo.
  • You do the task in IntelliJ, then tell me “done”. I read your code, run your tests, and quiz you. We only advance when it's solid.
  • Keep the two reference cards open while you work.
